Brihadaranyaka Upanishad Verse 1.2.1

तस्यार्चत आपोऽजायन्त; अर्चते वै मे कमभूदिति

As (death) was worshipping, water was produced.
(Since death thought), ‘As I was worshipping, water sprang up’

Rain today?
Rain comes from the east?
Rain comes from the south?
Rain comes from the west?
Rain comes from the north?

Reciters: Calling a storm at any time, he stays at his palace in the sky.

Sumur Wasiat

Peziarah datang dari berbagai kota. Mereka membaca tahlil dan memanjatkan doa untuk keselamatan, kesehatan, keberkahan, dan sebagainya.
Mereka membacakannya tepat di depan situs Sunan Kalijaga. Setelah berdoa mereka biasanya mengambil air keramat dari Sumur Wasiat.
Ada etika yang harus dijaga oleh peziarah. Tidak boleh berkata kotor dan merendahkan. Tentu, ini demi menjaga kesakralan tempat ini.
Saat musim kemarau ini mereka tidak bisa mengambilnya. Sumur ini mengalami kekeringan dan sekarang kondisinya ditutup. Meski demikian, tidak mengurangi semangat peziarah untuk tetap datang ke tempat ini.

Pilgrims come from various cities. They recite tahlil and offer prayers for safety, good health, blessings, and so on.
They recite the prayers right in front of the site of Sunan Kalijaga. After praying, they usually collect sacred water from the Sumur Wasiat (well).
There is an etiquette that pilgrims are expected to observe. They are not allowed to use foul or disrespectful language. Of course, this is to preserve the sanctity of the site.
During the current dry season, they are unable to collect the water. The well has dried up and is currently closed. Even so, this has not diminished the pilgrims’ enthusiasm to continue visiting the site.
